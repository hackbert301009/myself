The CV & AI Knowledge Lab is a German-language interactive knowledge hub built with Streamlit, bringing together more than 33 learning modules across computer vision and artificial intelligence in one place.

The modules span the full range from mathematical foundations through to modern architectures, covering transformers, diffusion models and multimodal AI. Each topic is accompanied by live demos so concepts can be explored hands-on rather than only read about.

Built in Python with Streamlit, the project is aimed at making the underlying ideas of CV and AI approachable and explorable for a German-speaking audience.
