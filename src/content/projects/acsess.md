ACSESS, the Adaptive Conversational Social Engineering Simulation System, is a fully local research prototype for studying AI-driven conversational tactics. It runs entirely on a local machine and is intended strictly for consent-based experiments.

The project investigates how language models adapt conversational strategies, with the goal of informing security-awareness training rather than enabling real-world manipulation. All experiments are framed around explicit consent and controlled conditions.

Implemented in Python on top of an LLM, ACSESS sits firmly in the research category as an exploratory tool for understanding and defending against social-engineering techniques.
